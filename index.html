How to Play

  • Move: tap empty ground. A yellow ring shows where your tap landed.
  • Attack: tap a zombie (generous hit area).
  • Gather: tap trees, bushes, crates, scrap.
  • Base: open Build while inside a building — claim it, set type (Safehouse, Workshop, Storage, Kitchen), board windows, reinforce doors & walls.
  • Skills: Combat, Foraging, Crafting, Fitness, Construction.
  • Death: skills kept; bag drops. Storage stash survives. Respawn at Safehouse or campfire.

Original homage to classic top-down skill games — not affiliated with Jagex or The Indie Stone.
