How to Play

  • Move: tap empty ground to walk there.
  • Attack: tap a zombie when in range (red ring).
  • Gather: tap trees, crates, or bushes when close.
  • Survive: manage health & hunger; eat food from the bag.
  • Skills: Combat, Foraging, Crafting, Fitness gain XP as you play.
  • Death: you keep skill levels; carried items drop as a loot pile.
  • Campfire: your respawn point — stay near it early on.

This is a solo vertical slice. Multiplayer is planned later; the code separates state/update/render for that.
