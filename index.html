How to Play

  • Tap: default action (walk / attack / gather). Yellow ring shows the registered tap.
  • Long-press (or right-click): action menu — Attack, Chop/Mine/Fish, Pick up, Examine, Walk here.
  • Skills: Craft, Smith, Build, Equip tabs. Recipes show owned/needed mats; locked ones are grey.
  • Tools: bronze/iron/steel axe & pickaxe gate trees and rocks.
  • Combat: swords melee; shortbow + arrows for Ranged. Armor reduces damage.
  • Creatures: zombies, rats, goblins (hostile); squirrels & chickens flee.
  • Buildings: tap ENTER door for interiors; claim & furnish (bed, forge, bank chest…).
  • Bank / Shop: public bank north of camp; Mira the Trader sells gear for coins.
  • Death: skills kept; gear drops. Bank contents persist.
